## Gate controls (no automatic progression)

| Gate | Present evidence | Owner decision needed | Prohibited now |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0: public research | docs/market-evidence.md + verified private repository | None for read-only work | False source, fabricated volume |
| G1: user need/differentiation | Real posts and competitor evidence, **NOT confirmed differentiation** | Market GO/NO-GO after independent tests | Public claims, revenue forecast |
| G2: budgeted product work | Private local prototype only | Explicit Founder record of time, cost, scope | Paid expansion, multiple tools |
| G3: production release | No real deployment, URL, privacy review or real end-user tests | Explicit owner approval and service-specific permissions | Public deployment, custom domain, indexing |
| G4: advertisement/commercial | No site approval, account, ad revenue, tax or payout evidence | Separate compliance and monetization approval | Ads, buying traffic, scraping, false click events |

This is a **local research-only** site. Even a correct calculator, noindex metadata and private repo cannot establish product-market fit or Google eligibility.
